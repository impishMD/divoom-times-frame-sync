Divoom Times Frame Sync runs as a single worker.

Follow its logs:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -f deployment/{{ include "tfs.fullname" . }}

The Pod needs outbound access to {{ .Values.config.frame.host }}:{{ .Values.config.frame.port }}, DNS, and your album services.
Create the destination albums in the Divoom app before syncing.
Restart the Deployment after rotating values in the external Secret.
Keep the data PVC between upgrades to preserve the sync journal.

Guide: https://github.com/impishMD/divoom-times-frame-sync/blob/main/docs/en/helm.md
